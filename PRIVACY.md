# Privacy

This describes what Decode It does with your data. It covers the plugin and the Worker in this repository, and the
hosted instance the project provides by default. If you run your own Worker (see
[docs/SELF_HOSTING.md](docs/SELF_HOSTING.md)), that deployment is yours and you are responsible for it.

*This is a plain-language description, not legal advice. Maintainers: complete the "Who runs the hosted instance" section before the repository goes public.*

## Short version

- In **live mode**, pressing **Agree and decode** sends the design image, your feedback and any optional notes to the
  Decode It Worker, which sends them to Google Gemini to generate suggestions.
- **Please avoid confidential or sensitive information** in designs, screenshots, feedback and notes.
- The Worker **does not store** your content, and our code logs status codes only. The services it runs on have their
  own handling, described below.
- **Demo mode** sends nothing anywhere.
- No analytics, no cookies, no accounts, no advertising.

## What is sent, and when

Only when you press the decode button in live mode, and only after you have pressed **Agree and decode** once:

| Data | Source | Sent to |
|---|---|---|
| An image of the selected frame (a PNG scaled to at most 1440 x 4800 px) or the screenshot you added | Figma selection or your upload | Worker, then Gemini |
| The feedback text (up to 1,000 characters) | What you typed or pasted | Worker, then Gemini |
| Optional notes: what the design is, who it is for, what it should do | The "A little more context" fields | Worker, then Gemini |
| The design's name and size (for example "Pricing page, 390 x 640") | Figma or the file name | Worker, then Gemini |

Nothing else is read from your Figma file. The plugin does not read other frames, comments, or file content, and does
not change your document. A frame is exported only when you select it, to show the preview.

Your **IP address** is visible to the Worker (and Cloudflare) because that is how internet requests work. The Worker uses
it only as the key for the rate limiter.

## What the Worker does with it

The Worker (`worker/`) validates the request, builds a prompt, calls the Gemini API and returns the result. It keeps no
database and writes no files. It does not log the image, the feedback or the notes. The only thing it logs is a status
code when something fails. You can read the code to confirm this.

## Third parties

- **Cloudflare** runs the Worker. Cloudflare processes requests as part of providing the service and can show request
  metadata (such as URL, status and time) in the Worker's dashboard if observability is enabled, which it is in the
  default configuration. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
- **Google (Gemini API)** receives your image, feedback and notes to produce the readings. How Google handles prompts
  depends on the plan of the API key used. **On Gemini's free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products, and
  humans may review them. Paid usage is treated differently.** Check Google's current Gemini API terms for the key you use.
  The project's hosted instance's key tier is stated in the section below.

## What stays on your device

- Whether you have pressed **Agree and decode** is remembered so you are not asked again. In Figma it is stored with
  `figma.clientStorage` on your computer, and in a plain browser it falls back to `localStorage`.
- The plugin keeps no history of your feedback or results. Closing the plugin discards them.
- The result can be copied to your clipboard when you press **Copy summary**. That copy stays with you.

## AI output

Results are generated by an AI model. They are suggestions and guesses, can be wrong or miss things, and should not be
treated as facts about what a reviewer meant. Ask the reviewer when it matters.

## Your choices

- Use **Demo mode** to avoid sending anything.
- Decline by not pressing **Agree and decode**. The plugin sends nothing until you do.
- Run your own Worker with your own key to control where your data goes.
